About the Lab

The Computer Vision and Intelligent Perception (CVIP) Lab is a world-class research group dedicated to advancing the state-of-the-art in artificial intelligence, computer vision, and machine learning. Our mission is to develop intelligent systems that can see, reason, and interact with the complex, dynamic world around us.

Founded by Prof. [Your Name], our lab fosters a collaborative and innovative environment, bringing together talented researchers from diverse backgrounds. We tackle fundamental challenges and drive real-world applications across various domains.

Research Focus

Medical Image Segmentation & CV

Specializing in multi-modal medical image segmentation (thyroid nodules, nuclei, vessels) and domain generalization techniques.

Multi-modal AI & Medical LLMs

Developing collaborative multi-agent systems and multi-modal fusion techniques to advance intelligent medical diagnosis.

AI-driven Diagnosis & Health

Exploring clinical applications of AI in mental health, nuclear medicine, and computational health solutions.
